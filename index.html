How I build and ship the work

I run the work as a team of AI coding agents with rules a reviewer can audit. I keep the decisions: architecture, accuracy and what goes live.

  1. One coordinator, many workers

    A coordinating session splits each job and hands it to worker sessions. Every worker writes a handoff; nothing is merged from memory.

  2. Facts follow a source ladder

    Product claims trace to datasheets and product data. Anything without a source is cut before it ships.

  3. Staging first, then production

    Changes go to staging and through automated checks first. Nothing reaches production without my approval.

  4. A second reader for every page

    A second model reviews wording and audits pages; its output is treated as a lead to check, never as a source.
