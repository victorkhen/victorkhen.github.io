About Victor Khen.

Who is Victor Khen?

Victor Khen is a partner and technical marketing professional at IP Infusion, working across partner go-to-market for open networking, telecom and AI infrastructure in the Americas, EMEA and APAC. His work combines ecosystem programs, product positioning, technical storytelling and hands-on execution. He previously held marketing, channel sales and partner roles at UfiSpace, QSAN, V2PLUS (D-Link) and ACTi.

What did Victor Khen build at IP Infusion?

His work spans sales, partner ecosystems, marketing, technical product marketing, SEO/AEO, web platforms and tool engineering. At IP Infusion, the website and OcNOS evaluation tools are examples of hands-on execution, not the whole of his role.

What is Netdryrun?

Netdryrun is an independent open-source network validation project. It runs checks against lab topologies to help identify routing and reachability issues before network changes are deployed. Its test coverage and limitations are documented in the public repository.

How does Victor Khen use AI in engineering work?

He uses AI coding agents to accelerate implementation, while retaining responsibility for architecture, product accuracy, testing and release decisions. His workflows include automated checks, staged releases and production safeguards. He applies this approach to practical digital tools and to Netdryrun, an independent open-source network validation project.

What networking topics does Victor Khen write about?

AI and data center fabrics (RoCEv2, lossless Ethernet, EVPN-VXLAN), service provider transport (SR-MPLS, SRv6, EVPN-MPLS), broadband and mobile access, IPoDWDM, merchant silicon, and the business case for disaggregated, open networking.
